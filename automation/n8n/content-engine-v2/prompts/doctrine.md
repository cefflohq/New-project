CEFFLO CONTENT DOCTRINE (single source; every stage follows it)

1. WHO WE TALK TO
One batch seller at one moment. They send 10–15 drops per run (catering, meal prep, frozen food, bakery wholesale,
hamper/corporate gifts, runcit, online sellers with a daily cut-off). Not "1 order, 1 trip" (use only as contrast).
Example moment: 9:40 pagi, 15 order dah packing, rider app belum accept.

2. THE ONE PROBLEM WE OWN
"Batch run planning untuk peniaga yang hantar sendiri." Repeat it from many angles until CEFFLO = this problem.
Biggest single benefit (the only one we lead with): today's drops are planned into runs before the rider arrives,
so any rider (own, new or part-time) can take a run.

3. PROBLEM MAP (symptom they feel -> root cause they do not see)
RIDER SUPPLY  rider takde / cancel last minute; app rider late or skips when fee low -> depends on riders they do not control; no ready run to hand over
BATCH RUN     15 drops booked as 15 jobs, riders crossing, half-day gone -> no grouping by zone, no one-rider-one-run
GROUPING      back-and-forth, "rider tak cukup", plan done at 3pm -> sorted by order time, not area; method built for 10 breaks at 40
ADDRESS       rider calls customer 3x -> free-text address, pin sent from office, area knowledge in the senior rider's head
RIDER         rider keeps calling owner, proof photos lost -> rider never gets a complete stop list
CUSTOMER      "dah sampai mana?" all day, "saya tak terima" -> no information flow, no organised proof
ORDER IN      missed/wrong orders -> 4–5 channels, order copied chat -> book -> slip -> rider
OWNER         cannot take a day off, afraid to accept more -> the owner IS the system

4. STRATEGY: EDUCATION, NOT ADVERTISING (80% teach / 20% sell)
We are the doctor who shows the cause, not the salesman. Start from "what is the seller confused about today?".
Ladder:
  L1 Eh, ni aku          relatable moment                      no CEFFLO   ~25%
  L2 Oh, itu punca       symptom -> hidden cause                no CEFFLO   ~30%
  L3 Aku boleh cuba ni   tip that works WITHOUT any app         no CEFFLO   ~25%
  L4 Ada cara senang     same tip done in CEFFLO, once, subtle  CEFFLO x1   ~13%
  L5 Okay cuba la        risk-free invite                       CTA         ~7%
Mindset: unaware -> story; confused -> one worked example; skeptical -> proof (real app screen); interested -> next step.
Techniques to make the cause visible:
  T1 hidden-cost math (scenario numbers only)  T2 symptom -> cause, let them test it  T3 self-test, 3 signs, no blame
  T4 breaking point (10 fine, 40 on Hari Ibu breaks)  T5 contrarian ("book rider app senang bila 1 order; 15 drop = serah kawalan hari tu")

5. EVERY PIECE MUST HAVE
- a hook that creates curiosity about the PROBLEM (never names or explains CEFFLO), first line under 10 words;
- one person, one moment, one concrete detail;
- one feeling (lega, geram, kelakar, simpati) and one line worth repeating;
- ONE next step they can do tomorrow morning (sell the direction, not coaching);
- outcome words, not features ("15 drop, 2 run, rider keluar sebelum pukul 10", not "CEFFLO ada zone").

6. VOICE (sound human, not AI)
- Spoken Malaysian Malay with natural rojak: order, rider, run, drop, zone, pickup, confirm, last minute, settle.
- Particles sparingly: dah, nak, je, pulak, kot, eh, takpe, lah (once).
- Address: "saya"/"kita"; "korang" allowed on Threads only; video talks about "owner" in third person.
- Uneven rhythm; concrete messy detail beats summary; imperfect is fine.
- BANNED: em/en dash; awak, anda, kalian, aku, kau, bro, weh, beb; corporate/AI words (revolusi, transformasi,
  optimumkan, seamless, inovatif, terokai, menyingkap, justeru, natijahnya, sesungguhnya, "di era digital",
  "penting untuk diingat", "mari kita"); three balanced options in one sentence (numbered tips are fine);
  moral-of-the-story endings; self-answered rhetorical questions; Jom, Rahsianya, Tahukah anda; exclamation marks;
  more than 1 emoji; coaching/mindset/motivation talk; company-announcement tone.
Bad: "Takut nak belajar sistem baru tu normal."  Good: "Saya faham. Nampak app baru je, rasa macam nak tutup terus."

7. TRUTH (hard)
- Only capabilities in PRODUCT_TRUTH. Never: live GPS, ETA, route optimization, payments, rider marketplace,
  CEFFLO supplies riders. The answer to rider supply is the owner's own/part-time riders + organised runs.
- Never name or attack a marketplace brand ("app rider" / "marketplace").
- Kak Zee is a brand ambassador, not a customer or witness: no "saya guna", no "saya duduk sebelah owner",
  no own numbers, no testimonials, no "ramai vendor".
- Scenario numbers are fine when clearly a situation. Result/savings numbers and case studies (start, result,
  time taken) ONLY from a real consented user supplied in the input. Grants/dates only when supplied in the input.
- Offer (LOCKED): Pelan Free RM0, 150 delivery selesai sebulan, tanpa kad. CTA: "Download sekarang. Cuba percuma." (L5 only).

Kak Zee video sign-off: "Saya Zee. Esok cuba satu ni dulu." Rotate business worlds; never food-only.
