Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Bahasa Melayu

1. Who we are

Cefflo is operated by Cefflo Technologies (PG0592731-P), [business address — to be updated]. For any privacy question or request, email support@cefflo.com. This policy covers the Cefflo website, the Cefflo Vendor app and Vendor Web (business owners, operators and helpers), the Cefflo Driver app, Storefront ordering pages, driver invitation pages and customer tracking links.

2. Laws we follow

We process personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, including its notice, consent, security, retention, data-integrity, access and data-portability principles. If you are outside Malaysia, you also keep the rights your own country's law gives you, for example the EU/UK GDPR, Singapore's PDPA 2012, Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU 27/2022), Thailand's PDPA 2019 or the Philippines' Data Privacy Act 2012. Where those laws give you more protection, we apply it to you.

3. Our two roles

For accounts, team access, subscriptions and the Driver marketplace, Cefflo decides how data is used (data user / controller). For delivery orders, the business you order from decides how your order data is used and Cefflo processes it on that business's behalf (data processor). Each business is responsible for telling its own customers how it uses their data.

4. What we collect

Business owners, operators and helpers: name, email, phone, sign-in details (including Google sign-in), business details, role and actions taken. Drivers: name, phone, date of birth, address, emergency contact, vehicle type and plate, profile photo, schedule and jobs applied for, live location only while a run is active, proof-of-delivery photos and delivery records. Drivers using Find Jobs: identity card number (stored only as a protected code plus the last 4 digits, never in full), driving licence and vehicle photos. Customers: name, phone, delivery address, map pin and notes, items ordered, proof-of-delivery photo, rating and feedback. Everyone: device and app information needed for sign-in, notifications and security, and records of actions taken in the system. We do not collect biometric data.

5. Why we use it

To create and secure accounts; to plan, assign and complete deliveries; to show delivery progress to the business and its customer; to verify marketplace Drivers; to send service notifications (including push notifications); to provide support; to prevent fraud and misuse; to meet legal, tax and accounting duties; and to improve Cefflo using aggregated information. We rely on your consent, on performing our contract with you, on our legal obligations and on our legitimate interests in running a safe service.

6. Location data

The Driver app collects precise location only while a delivery run is active, including when the app is in the background, so the business and its customer can follow the delivery and so the pickup checklist can open when the Driver arrives at the pickup point. Location is not collected when no run is active. You can turn location permission off in your phone settings, but runs cannot be started without it.

7. Camera, photos and notifications

The apps use the camera only when you choose to: to scan an invitation QR code, to photograph proof of delivery, and to photograph documents for Driver verification. Push notifications are sent through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and only contain the information needed to tell you about your work; you can turn them off at any time.

8. Who we share it with

We share data only as needed to run Cefflo: the business you work with or order from and its team; a Driver sees only the stops assigned to them; whoever holds a tracking link sees that order's status. Our service providers: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (web hosting), Google (sign-in with Google, Maps, Places and Firebase Cloud Messaging). They may only use the data to provide their service to us. We may disclose data where the law requires it. We never sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

9. Transfers outside Malaysia

Our providers may store or process data outside Malaysia (our database is planned to be hosted in Singapore). We transfer data only to providers bound by contract to protect it to a standard comparable to Malaysian law, as permitted by section 129 of the PDPA.

10. How long we keep it

Accounts: while active and 30 days after closure. Orders and delivery records: 7 years, for tax and accounting duties. Proof-of-delivery photos: 12 months. Driver live location: 30 days. Driver verification documents: while the account is active and 30 days after. Security logs: 12 months. After these periods data is deleted or anonymised.

11. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, limit or object to processing, or receive your data in a portable format. Email support@cefflo.com; we reply within 21 days. For a delivery order you can also contact the business you ordered from. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Commissioner, or to the authority in your own country.

12. Security and breaches

Access is restricted by role, documents are kept in private storage, identity numbers are never stored in full, connections are encrypted and administrator access requires two-factor authentication. If a breach is likely to cause significant harm, we notify the Commissioner within 72 hours and affected people without undue delay, as the 2024 amendment requires.

13. Children

Cefflo is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

14. Changes

We will post updates here with a new date and tell account holders in the app about material changes.
