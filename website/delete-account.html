Delete your Cefflo account

Cefflo Vendor and Cefflo Driver · Bahasa Melayu

In the app

Cefflo Driver: Profile → Delete account (under Log Out).
Cefflo Vendor: More → Delete account (under Sign out).

Your account closes straight away: you are signed out on every device and cannot sign in again.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@cefflo.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We close it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and when

DataWhat happens
Name, phone, email, profile photo, sign-inErased 30 days after your request
Driver licence, IC and vehicle photosErased 30 days after your request
Order and delivery recordsKept 7 years for tax and accounting law, without your name
Proof-of-delivery photosDeleted 12 months after the delivery

If you own a business, its team loses access when your account closes. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Padam akaun Cefflo anda

Dalam app Cefflo Driver: Profil → Padam akaun.
Dalam app Cefflo Vendor: More → Padam akaun.
Akaun ditutup serta-merta. Jika tidak dapat membuka app, emel support@cefflo.com dari alamat emel akaun anda dengan tajuk “Padam akaun saya”; kami tutup dalam 7 hari. Nama, telefon, emel dan dokumen dipadam 30 hari selepas permintaan. Rekod order disimpan 7 tahun tanpa nama anda, seperti yang diwajibkan undang-undang.
