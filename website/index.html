FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Cefflo?

Cefflo is an operating system for local same-day delivery. It brings your orders, delivery zones, multi-drop runs, riders and customer tracking into one place, for businesses that deliver their own orders.

Is Cefflo a delivery marketplace?

No. Cefflo is not a marketplace, courier or aggregator. Your customers, your sales channels and your riders stay yours. Cefflo is the system you run your delivery operation on.

Do I need my own riders?

Yes. Cefflo is for businesses that deliver with their own riders or drivers. You invite them to your team, approve them and send them runs, which they work through on their phone.

How does customer tracking work?

Each order has a tracking link you share with your customer. No app and no account — once the delivery starts they see the order go from picked up to on the way to delivered, with proof of delivery at the end.

What types of businesses is Cefflo for?

Local businesses that run their own delivery with many orders a day: F&B, bakeries, meal prep, florists, gifts and hampers, beauty and other local retail.

How do I get started?

The Cefflo app is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Once it's out, you download it, set up your zones and invite your riders.
