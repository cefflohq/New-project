FAQ

Real questions, clear answers.

What is Cefflo?

Cefflo is an operating system for local businesses that run their own delivery.

Is Cefflo a delivery company?

No. Cefflo is not a courier or marketplace. Your customers, sales channels and Drivers stay yours.

Do I need my own Drivers?

Yes. You invite your own Drivers and assign runs to them.

Can customers order through Cefflo?

Yes. Cefflo Storefront provides an ordering page connected to your operation.

Can customers track deliveries?

Yes. They open a tracking link without needing an app or account.

Can my staff use Cefflo?

Yes. Operators and Helpers have task-appropriate access, and Drivers use their own workspace.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The FREE plan includes up to 150 completed deliveries per month.

Do I need a website?

No. You can use Cefflo Storefront or enter orders from channels you already use.
