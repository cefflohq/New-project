2. Two roles

For accounts, subscriptions and the Driver marketplace, Cefflo decides how data is used. For delivery orders, the business you order from decides how your order data is used, and Cefflo processes it on that business's behalf.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run Cefflo: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (sign-in with Google). The business you order from and its team see the order data they need. A Driver sees only the stops assigned to them. Your tracking link shows status only to whoever holds the link. We do not sell personal data.
