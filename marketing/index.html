Local density, batched into same-day delivery

Cefflo turns a list of orders into an organized delivery operation, built around the area your business already covers. Three moments make up the whole model:

Coverage to Zones

Your area, split the way you actually operate

You define the area you can realistically serve. Inside it, nearby orders are grouped into zones, not treated as separate, isolated trips.

Plan Route to Pickup Checklist to Delivery Run

One rider, one run, every stop accounted for

Each zone becomes a delivery run: the route is planned, pickups are checked off one by one, and the rider works through the drops, reordering their own stops as needed, until every order in that zone is delivered.

Placed to Delivered

Before the day is over

An order placed in the morning is in a run by midday and delivered the same day, with live status shared automatically, so nobody has to ask.

Built for businesses with local order density

Cefflo fits best when you already generate your own orders or customer demand, a meaningful share of your customers sit within a reachable local area, and you operate or coordinate your own riders, whether that's staff, family, or a trusted delivery team. If you want to batch nearby orders into multi-drop runs instead of one-off trips, and delivery speed can genuinely help you win against competitors, this is built for you.

Common questions

What is Cefflo?
Cefflo is a local same-day delivery operating system. It helps a business organize its own orders into zone-based, multi-drop delivery runs, with a dashboard to manage it, your own riders to run it, and live tracking for your customers.
Is Cefflo a marketplace?
No. Cefflo doesn't generate customer demand or put you inside a public marketplace. You keep the customers and orders you already have. Cefflo helps you operate the delivery side of that business.
Do I need my own riders to use Cefflo?
Yes. Cefflo is built around your own rider fleet, whether that's one part-time rider or a team of twenty. The Rider app helps them run their zone, and your dashboard helps you manage them.
How is Cefflo different from Grab, foodpanda, or Lalamove?
Grab and foodpanda take a commission on every order and own your customer database, not you. Lalamove charges per delivery trip with no zone or batching concept. Cefflo groups your own local orders into zones and multi-drop runs your own riders complete, for a flat monthly fee.
Can my customers track their orders?
Yes. Every order comes with a live tracking link and its own Customer app experience, from "Picked Up" through "On The Way" to "Delivered."
Can I customize my Storefront to match my brand?
Yes. Your order page uses your business name and logo, and you choose from curated themes for background and typography. The ordering flow stays consistent so it's always recognizably yours, and always works.
Can orders come from somewhere other than my Cefflo Storefront?
Yes. You can add orders manually if you're still selling through WhatsApp or Instagram DMs. Bulk import from a spreadsheet is coming next.
What happens after my free trial ends?
You'll be notified before your trial ends. If you don't upgrade, your account automatically continues on Cefflo Go. You won't be charged without confirming first.
