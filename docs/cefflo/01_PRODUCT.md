# CEFFLO --- PRODUCT

## P-00 Identity

Cefflo is an **Operating System for Home-Based Food Businesses**.
Tagline: **Operate Smarter, Deliver Better.**

## P-01 Problem

Home-based food operators often handle sales, orders, fulfillment, rider
coordination, delivery troubleshooting and customer communication
themselves. Cefflo reduces this operational chaos through one workflow.

## P-02 Not Cefflo

Cefflo is not primarily: - a marketplace; - a GrabFood-style consumer
discovery app; - a rider company; - an on-demand rider marketplace; - a
generic delivery company; - a generic SaaS dashboard.

## P-03 Primary Customer

Acquisition focus: home-based food vendors, including bakeries,
catering, frozen food, meal prep, desserts, preorder food and similar
home food businesses.

## P-04 Ownership

Vendor controls products, prices, customer acquisition, customer
relationships, sales channels, order acceptance, delivery policy and
rider team. Cefflo provides operating infrastructure.

## P-05 Rider Strategy

Current model supports vendor-owned/trusted riders: staff, part-time,
freelance, family or trusted helpers. Cefflo does not currently depend
on recruiting a proprietary rider marketplace.

## P-06 Delivery Strategy

Support planned operations: batching, zones, one rider/multiple drops,
routes, stops, assignments, progress, exceptions and POD. Real-world
issues include flats, condo access, wrong addresses, unreachable
customers, rural distance, vendor-not-ready and time-window pressure.

## P-07 Sales Direction

Vendors may receive orders from their own customers through
vendor-controlled sales/order pages and shared links. Cefflo should not
take ownership of the vendor's customer channel.

## P-08 Payments

Current direction: vendor-controlled direct payment such as QR/FPX where
applicable. COD is not a core model; riders should not become Cefflo
cash handlers.

## P-09 Automation

Automate repetitive work while preserving meaningful human decisions.
Future areas may include manual/CSV/Google Sheets intake, mapping,
validation, deduplication, batching, zones, routes, assignment,
tracking, POD, history and rider performance.

## P-10 UX Principle

Mobile-first, clean, minimal, operationally clear. Action hierarchy
beats decorative complexity.

## P-11 Scale Direction

Planning targets: 5,000+ vendors, 50,000+ riders, 500,000+
customer-tracking visitors. Use scalable foundations without premature
over-engineering.

## P-12 Immediate Goal

Immediate objective: Stage 4 production readiness. Do not delay Stage 4
by prematurely implementing later-stage native apps, regional expansion
or unnecessary AI automation.
