# CEFFLO — Set 1 Hero Video Script (DRAFT)

Status: DRAFT for Founder review. Not approved for production or publishing.
Sources: `sot/05_BRAND_BRAIN.md` (§16, §20, §32–35, §39, B4),
`sot/marketing/10_BRAND_VOICE_LANGUAGE_SYSTEM.md`, `sot/01_PRODUCT_TRUTH.md` §18.

- Length: ~75 s master (cut-downs: 30 s, 15 s)
- Formats: 16:9 master; 9:16 and 4:5 reframes
- Language: Malaysian Malay (narrator Register A, characters Register B/C)
- Story model: one order (#1048 — Nurul — Brownie Box — Shah Alam) followed end to end
- Scenario: busy bakery / packed-food kitchen, multi-drop runs (not a florist)

## Claim guardrails (Product Truth §18)

Shown as LIVE and allowed: bulk CSV/Excel import, suggested runs + Run Builder,
rider stop resequencing, coarse ETA window, photo POD, customer tracking page.

Do NOT show or say: live rider GPS on customer map, WhatsApp/SMS notifications,
"sync" with any channel, AI/optimization claims, any number presented as
real results (time saved, businesses, riders). All UI numbers are illustrative.

---

## Scene 1 — The mess (0:00–0:10)

**VISUAL:** 7:30 a.m. bakery kitchen. Trays of brownie boxes. Owner's phone buzzes
non-stop: WhatsApp chats, a notebook of addresses, a spreadsheet open on a laptop.

**OWNER (Register B, to herself):**
> "Ada 60 lebih order hari ni. Rider tiga je... nak bahagi macam mana ni?"

**SUPER:** *Many orders.*

## Scene 2 — Staff pressure (0:10–0:16)

**VISUAL:** Packing staff holding two bags, looking at handwritten labels.

**STAFF (Register C):**
> "Kak, yang Shah Alam dengan Klang ni letak sekali ke?"

**OWNER:**
> "Kejap... kejap."

## Scene 3 — Into one place (0:16–0:24)

**VISUAL:** Owner imports the day's orders into Cefflo Vendor Web. Today's Orders
fills up. Camera finds one row: **#1048 · Nurul · Brownie Box · Shah Alam**.

**NARRATOR (Register A):**
> "Semua order hari ni, dalam satu tempat."

**SUPER:** *One operation.*

## Scene 4 — Zones and runs (0:24–0:36)

**VISUAL:** Orders cluster into delivery zones. Suggested runs appear.
**Run Shah Alam A1 — 12 stops.** #1048 sits at Stop 3. Owner reviews, assigns to Rider Amir.

**NARRATOR:**
> "Susun ikut zone. Semak run. Assign rider."

## Scene 5 — Team works from the same truth (0:36–0:44)

**VISUAL:** Packing staff now see the run list on screen; bags are grouped and
labelled per run. A1 bags lined up together.

**STAFF:**
> "Okay, A1 dah siap. Dua belas beg."

## Scene 6 — One rider, one run, many stops (0:44–0:56)

**VISUAL:** Amir collects the full A1 batch. Cefflo Driver on his phone: ordered stop
list, Stop 3 of 12. Quick montage of drops across Shah Alam.

**RIDER (brief, respectful):**
> "Stop tiga, Nurul. Okay."

**SUPER:** *One rider. One run. Many stops.*

## Scene 7 — Customer informed (0:56–1:04)

**VISUAL:** Nurul at home opens her tracking link: order status and an estimated
delivery window (a window, not an exact minute; no live rider map).
Doorbell. Amir hands over the box, takes a photo for POD.

**NARRATOR:**
> "Customer tahu order dia sampai mana. Bukti delivery pun ada."

## Scene 8 — The day under control (1:04–1:12)

**VISUAL:** Owner back at the kitchen, calmer. Screen shows the day's runs progressing,
#1048 marked **Delivered**. She finally sips her coffee.

**NARRATOR:**
> "Banyak order. Satu operation. Sampai hari ni."

## Scene 9 — End card (1:12–1:15)

**VISUAL:** Cefflo logo.

**SUPER:** **Run local delivery. Without the chaos.**
**CTA:** *Urus delivery hari ni dengan Cefflo.* + URL
