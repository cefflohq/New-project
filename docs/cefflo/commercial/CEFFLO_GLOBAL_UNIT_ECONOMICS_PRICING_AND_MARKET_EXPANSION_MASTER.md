# 12. Pricing Simplicity Test

A pricing model can be economically optimal but commercially poor if customers cannot understand it.

For every candidate model evaluate:

- pricing clarity;
- predictability;
- ease of budgeting;
- risk of bill shock;
- fairness to seasonal/part-time Rider operations;
- fairness to high-volume/low-Rider operations;
- fairness to high-Rider/low-volume operations;
- ability for Cefflo revenue to scale with customer value;
- ability to protect margin.

Do not recommend complexity merely because it maximizes theoretical revenue.
