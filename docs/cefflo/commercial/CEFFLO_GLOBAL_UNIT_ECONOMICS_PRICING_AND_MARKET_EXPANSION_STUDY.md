## A. Executive summary

1. **Cefflo's variable cost follows delivery work, not rider headcount.**
   - Every metered unit in the current system is generated by an order, a
     run, a movement-gated location write or a customer viewing a tracking
     link.
   - A rider who exists but is not on an active run generates **no** location
     writes, Realtime traffic or tracking reads (D-66 design; code: writes
     only via `record_rider_location` during active assignments).
   - (CALCULATED from architecture.)
2. **Case A (3 riders, 4,000 deliveries/month) costs Cefflo more than Case B
   (15 riders, 500 deliveries/month).**
   - Per-order units (geocoding, RPC writes, POD objects, tracking reads)
     scale ×8.
   - Rider-activity units scale with active run time, which follows
     deliveries rather than headcount.
   - Rider count does **not** materially change COGS on its own.
3. **This supports existing Pricing Master principles:**
   - P-05: completed delivery is the primary usage metric.
   - P-04: do not price per rider.

   A rider-based price would track a quantity that barely drives Cefflo's
   cost.
4. **Margin thresholds.** At full allowance, the candidate prices imply the
   following ceilings on COGS per completed delivery (CALCULATED):
   - OPERATE (RM199 / 1,500): **≤ RM0.027** for 80% gross margin.
   - SCALE (RM499 / 5,000): **≤ RM0.020** for 80% gross margin.
   - GROW (RM99 / 500): **≤ RM0.040** for 80% gross margin.
   - FREE (RM0 / 100): the whole cost is acquisition spend.

   The Founder can test these against real provider prices without new
   modelling.
5. **Worldwide readiness is PARTIAL.**
   - **Language:** READY (BM/EN, per-user, device-first).
   - **Timezone and currency:** exist as per-business fields, but the UI
     hard-codes "RM" and new businesses default to MYR / Asia/Kuala_Lumpur.
   - **Missing:** there is **no business-country field**, no price book and no
     multi-country payment path (Curlec is the Malaysian launch gateway).
   - None of these blocks a Malaysia launch; all must precede a second
     country.
6. **Evidence sufficient to reconsider pricing: NO.** That needs recorded
   provider prices plus production telemetry (§Y).

## F. Run cost model (units per run)

These are CALCULATED from §D. Location writes scale with run duration and
movement; this model assumes about 8.5 per drop (ESTIMATE).

| Drops | DB row writes | RPC calls | Geocodes | Location writes | Tracking reads | POD objects |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | ~140 | ~62 | 5 | ~43 | ~27 | 5 |
| 10 | ~280 | ~122 | 10 | ~85 | ~53 | 10 |
| 15 | ~420 | ~182 | 15 | ~128 | ~80 | 15 |
| 20 | ~560 | ~242 | 20 | ~170 | ~106 | 20 |

- **Multi-drop effect.** The only per-run (not per-drop) units are run build,
  capacity check and run start. Everything else is per drop, so cost per drop
  is roughly flat with run size.
- **Location writes.** Denser runs mean fewer kilometres per drop, so the
  movement-gated gate writes somewhat fewer location rows per drop.
- **Rider pay** is excluded: under the current model the Vendor owns and pays
  its riders.

## H. Vendor scenario matrix (units per month)

Units are CALCULATED at 30 working days (×26/30 for 26-day months). The model
assumes about 27 DB writes, 1 geocode and about 8.5 location writes per
delivery.

| Scenario | Riders | Orders/day | Deliveries/mo | DB writes/mo | Geocodes/mo | Location writes/mo | POD objects/mo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Micro | 1 | 10 | 300 | ~8 K | 300 | ~2.6 K | 300 |
| Small | 3 | 50 | 1,500 | ~41 K | 1,500 | ~13 K | 1,500 |
| Growing | 10 | 200 | 6,000 | ~162 K | 6,000 | ~51 K | 6,000 |
| High volume | 30 | 1,000 | 30,000 | ~810 K | 30,000 | ~255 K | 30,000 |

| **Case B** | 15 | ~17 | 500 | ~14 K | 500 | ~4.3 K* | 500 |

×8 on every per-order stream. The difference comes from geocoding,
DB writes, POD storage/egress and tracking reads. Rider count does not
materially change COGS independently of volume (CALCULATED).

## I. Marginal vs allocated full cost

- **Marginal cost per delivery.** This is §G: units × unit price, with no
  fixed share. It is UNKNOWN until §C is filled.
- **Allocated full cost.** Add the fixed platform (Supabase plan, hosting,
  domains, SMTP base, logging) ÷ total completed deliveries (or ÷ vendors for
  cost per vendor). Fixed cost is UNKNOWN. The allocation formula follows the
  brief §9 and does **not** divide bills by rider count.
- Until real volume exists, allocated cost per delivery is dominated by the
  fixed platform. It falls with every vendor added.

## K–N. Pricing model simulations

The three models are compared structurally, not in money, because COGS is
UNKNOWN.

| Model | Revenue tracks | Cost tracks | Alignment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: delivery/order-based (current candidate) | completed deliveries | per-delivery units (§D) | **High** | Matches P-05; bill-shock risk handled by P-06 and plan steps |
| B: active-rider-based | rider count | ~Auth MAU only | **Low** | Penalises seasonal/part-time riders; under-charges high-volume, low-rider vendors (Case A). Conflicts with P-04. Not approved. |
| C: hybrid | riders + deliveries | deliveries | Medium | More complex. Rider caps already exist as plan *boundaries* on low tiers (P-04 allows this) without per-rider billing. |

- **Gross margin comparison:** UNKNOWN in money.
- **Structurally**, Model A keeps revenue and cost moving together; Model B
  does not.
- **Nothing changes.** No model is recommended for adoption; this is a
  comparative study only.

### Simplicity test (brief §12)

- **Model A:** clear, predictable and budgetable, with fair treatment of
  seasonal riders. Weak point: high-volume, low-price vendors at the SCALE
  ceiling, which is handled by Enterprise.
- **Model B:** simple to read but unfair in both directions.
- **Model C:** hardest to explain.

## T. Country price-book architecture (conceptual; not implemented)

```text
price_book(
  id, market_code (ISO 3166-1), currency (ISO 4217),
  version, effective_from, effective_to,
  status: draft | active | retired

price_book_plan(
  price_book_id, plan_code (FREE|GROW|OPERATE|SCALE|…),
```

- Versioning protects historical subscriptions.
- There is no FX conversion.
- Only `MY / MYR` would exist, and only after the Founder locks prices.
- **No price is created for any other market.**

## X. Malaysia reference-market strategy

Malaysia is the **first operating and reference market**, not a disposable
test market.

> Real operations → measurable productivity/economics → credible reference
> businesses (especially F&B running own delivery) → repeatable playbook →
> adapt, not copy, into other markets.

No claim that Malaysia is already a regional standard; that must be earned in
outcomes.
