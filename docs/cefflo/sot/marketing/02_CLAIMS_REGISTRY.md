# CEFFLO — CLAIMS REGISTRY
**Status:** Canonical Marketing Safety & Capability SOT
**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-09-04
**Authority:** Product Truth controls capability reality.

## 1. Purpose
Prevent marketing, agents and creative tools from converting roadmap, mocks, backend foundations or assumptions into public product claims.

## 2. Claim Decision System
Every material claim is:
- **GREEN** — safe at current evidence level.
- **AMBER** — requires current verification/review before publication.
- **RED** — prohibited, unsupported, misleading or out of scope.

Capability lifecycle:
**LIVE / LOCKED-IN DEVELOPMENT / FUTURE / IDEA-EXPLORATION / OUT OF SCOPE**

Only verified LIVE capabilities may be described as currently available.

## 3. Evidence Hierarchy
Strongest to weakest:
1. verified current production/runtime behavior;
2. verified current staging/repository workflow with explicit launch-state wording;
3. canonical backend contract plus completed user-facing implementation evidence;
4. approved prototype clearly labeled prototype/preview;
5. roadmap/Founder-approved future direction;
6. idea/exploration.

Levels 4–6 cannot be represented as live product.

## 4. GREEN — Positioning Claims
Safe:
- "Cefflo is a local same-day delivery operating system for businesses that manage deliveries within their own service area."
- "Cefflo is built around orders, coverage, delivery zones, delivery plans, multi-drop runs, riders and delivered-today operations."
- "Cefflo is not a rider marketplace."
- "Cefflo is not a delivery marketplace."
- "Your business remains the customer-facing brand; Cefflo operates behind the workflow."
- "Cefflo is not limited to food businesses."
- "Cefflo is designed to make local delivery operations clearer."

Do not append unverified quantified outcomes.

## 5. GREEN — Product Doctrine
Safe as architecture/doctrine:
- Vendor/Owner = Control.
- Operations/Helper = Prepare.
- Rider = Execute.
- Customer = Order + Track.
- Product category is not the boundary; operating model is.
- Rider relationship remains vendor-owned in the current model.

Specific UI/features still require current-state verification.

## 6. AMBER — Must Verify Before Saying LIVE
Always verify current implementation/status:
- CSV/Excel/bulk import;
- address autocomplete/geocoding;
- coverage-area enforcement;
- deterministic planning;
- vehicle compatibility;
- rider capacity;
- route/stop sequencing;
- automatic optimization;
- AI optimization;
- real rider GPS;
- live map/location;
- ETA;
- WhatsApp/SMS notifications;
- recovery/reassignment;
- POS/API integrations;
- arbitrary storefront customization;
- e-Invoice/invoice;
- customer payment features;
- specific pricing;
- specific trial/launch availability;
- any performance or savings claim.

If verification is unavailable: rewrite without the capability or label it explicitly as preview/in development where Founder approves.

## 7. RED — Prohibited / Current Boundary
Do not claim:
- Cefflo supplies an independent rider network;
- Cefflo is like GrabFood/Foodpanda/Lalamove;
- Cefflo is a marketplace;
- Cefflo processes vendor-customer payments;
- Cefflo manages vendor-customer deposits/balances/settlements;
- Cefflo is an accounting platform;
- Cefflo is food-only/Home Food OS;
- AI actor is a genuine customer;
- fabricated testimonial/review;
- fabricated order/revenue/delivery numbers;
- fabricated time/cost savings;
- fabricated integration;
- fabricated GPS/ETA/live state;
- fake scarcity;
- unsupported "best/#1/faster/cheaper" superiority;
- prototype/mock screen presented as current production.

## 8. Optimization Language Rule
"Plan," "review," "sequence," and "reorder" are not automatically "optimize."

Use "optimized/optimization/AI route optimization" only when:
- the relevant capability is verified LIVE;
- the exact optimization behavior is known;
- limitations/human review are not hidden.

## 9. Real-Time Language Rule
Words such as:
- live;
- real-time;
- current location;
- ETA;
- tracking active

require real current data. Never infer them from UI animation, cached state or intended architecture.

## 10. Proof Claims
### Allowed
Real customer quote with consent; real measured result with methodology/context; real product capture; real usage data approved for publication.

### Not allowed
Composite testimonial presented as real; AI-generated "customer review"; invented numbers; stock/AI actor framed as customer proof.

UGC-style content may explain scenarios but is not customer proof.

## 11. Comparative Claims
Allowed:
- compare workflows factually;
- "spreadsheet records information; Cefflo is designed to connect delivery operations" where product truth supports it;
- explain differences from marketplace models.

Require review:
- named competitor comparison;
- price superiority;
- speed/performance superiority;
- market-leader claims.

Never fabricate competitor behavior.

## 12. Pricing & Availability
Pricing, trial, subscription and launch-state claims must be loaded from current approved commercial truth at generation time.

Never let old content hard-code an obsolete price or launch date.

## 13. Payment Boundary
Vendor-to-Cefflo SaaS subscription billing is separate from vendor-customer commerce.

Malaysia direction: Razorpay Curlec is the locked primary gateway for Cefflo's own SaaS subscription billing, subject to onboarding/integration/activation state.

Do not convert this into "Cefflo handles your customer payments."

## 14. Claim Record Schema
Each claim:
- claim_id;
- canonical wording;
- category;
- status GREEN/AMBER/RED;
- capability state;
- evidence source;
- evidence timestamp/version;
- allowed contexts;
- forbidden wording;
- expiry/recheck;
- Founder decision if applicable.

## 15. Generation Gate
For each content item:
1. extract claims;
2. map to claim IDs;
3. verify status/evidence;
4. PASS / REVISE / REJECT;
5. record decision.

Unknown material claim defaults to AMBER, not GREEN.

## 16. Visual Claim Gate
A visual can make a claim without words.

Check:
- UI shown;
- status shown;
- map/GPS/ETA;
- customer names/order volume;
- integrations/logos;
- analytics;
- notifications;
- "before/after" result.

Mockups must be labeled/used in a way that does not imply live capability.

## 17. Founder Gates
Founder approval required for:
- expanding master positioning;
- turning future/idea into committed public promise;
- major named-competitor claims;
- material quantified performance claims;
- genuine customer identity/testimonial use;
- new commercial/pricing claim where not already canonical.

## 18. Definition of Done
No normal marketing path can publish an unverified material capability claim without being flagged, and all public claims remain consistent with Product Truth.
