# CEFFLO — CREATIVE PLAYBOOK
**Status:** Canonical Creative Production SOT
**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-09-04

## 1. Purpose
Turn approved marketing experiments into recognizable, truthful, high-quality Cefflo creative without hard-coding one AI vendor or one visual style.

## 2. Creative Principle
**Creative serves the idea and evidence. Tools serve the creative.**

Do not choose "AI video" merely because an AI video provider exists.

Production Router chooses the lane from:
- communication objective;
- evidence available;
- platform;
- required realism;
- cost/time;
- brand fit.

## 4. Production Lanes
### L1 Real Product Demo
Best for feature understanding/trust.
Sources: real screen capture, approved prototype clearly framed, real UI footage.
Pattern: pain/hook → action → state change → operational meaning → CTA.

### L2 Founder / Real Human
Founder talking head, voiceover, hands-on product explanation, operational POV.
Keep natural. Avoid over-scripted "AI presenter" cadence.

### L3 UGC / Creator-Style Explainer
Use for relatable scenarios, objections, education.
AI/synthetic creator may act as narrator/character but must not impersonate a genuine customer or give fake testimonial.

### L4 Generative Scenario / B-roll
Use for visualizing operational situations, transitions, mood, establishing shots.
Do not use generated UI/product proof.

### L5 Static
One sharp idea, visual hierarchy, concise copy.

### L6 Carousel
Best for step-by-step, myth/reality, workflow, before/after, operational education.

### L7 Motion Graphic
Use diagrams, operational flows, UI callouts, kinetic text, state transitions.

### L8 Hybrid
Combine real product + human/voice + B-roll + captions/motion.

## 5. UGC Realism Rules
Natural UGC should use:
- believable environment;
- conversational delivery;
- imperfect-but-clean framing;
- natural pauses;
- realistic phone-camera perspective where suitable;
- non-corporate language;
- short sentences;
- one core point.

Avoid:
- uncanny perfect skin/motion;
- fake customer identity;
- scripted testimonial clichés;
- exaggerated reactions;
- "I made RM…"/"saved X hours" without real evidence;
- pretending generated footage is documentary evidence.

## 6. Short-Video Structures
### V1 Pain Reveal
Hook → recognizable situation → why it breaks → reframe → Cefflo/lesson → CTA.

### V2 Contrarian
Common belief → challenge → operational explanation → evidence → CTA.

### V3 Product Proof
Problem → real UI action → visible result/state → meaning.

### V4 Before / After
Before workflow → friction → after workflow → exact truthful difference.

### V5 Founder POV
Observation → lesson → product principle → relevance.

### V6 Objection
Quote objection → agree with valid part → expose threshold/gap → show fit/non-fit.

### V7 Micro Education
Question → 2–3 steps/principles → takeaway → optional CTA.

**Shot/timing cross-reference:** `09_CONTENT_WORLD_PRODUCTION_DOCTRINE.md` §9 (Shot-Level Production Doctrine — assembling from controlled real+generated shots rather than one monolithic AI generation) and §10 (Standard Short-Form Structure — 0–2s hook / 2–7s problem / 7–18s solution / 18–25s outcome / 25–30s CTA, 9:16 default) elaborate these structures for video production specifically.

## 7. Script Rules
- hook immediately;
- one core idea;
- spoken language, not essay language;
- remove filler;
- use operational nouns/verbs;
- show evidence when claiming product behavior;
- CTA proportional to funnel stage;
- do not force Cefflo into every first sentence.

**Voice/language cross-reference:** `10_BRAND_VOICE_LANGUAGE_SYSTEM.md` governs brand voice and Malaysian Malay generation in full — native generation only (never translated from an English master), speech-register selection (Clean Natural / Everyday Business / Pasar Beradab), the respect boundary (no `aku/kau/korang`/forced slang), and speaker-specific tone (brand narrator vs. owner vs. staff vs. rider vs. educational vs. product-demo voice). These script rules apply to both languages; language-specific naturalness is governed there.

## 8. Static Formula
- one dominant statement;
- one supporting visual/diagram/product fragment;
- one supporting line max where possible;
- brand mark restrained;
- Signal Lime only as meaningful signal.

## 9. Carousel Formula
Typical 5–8 frames:
1. hook;
2. situation;
3. why it happens;
4. operating principle;
5. example/product evidence;
6. implication;
7. CTA/discussion.

No requirement to use all frames.

## 10. Product Capture Rules
- use current approved build/prototype;
- hide/redact private/customer data;
- do not fabricate state;
- cursor/tap/motion should clarify action;
- crop for platform without obscuring truth;
- label preview/prototype when not live;
- never show a feature as functional solely because a mock exists.

## 11. Platform Adaptation

**Meta-shared-default (added 2026-09-10, see `docs/cefflo/05_DECISIONS.md` D-25):** Instagram and Facebook are packaged as **one shared Meta asset by default** (`08_AI_CONTENT_ENGINE_ORCHESTRATOR.md` §10) rather than two independently generated packages. The Instagram and Facebook subsections below describe platform-adaptation guidance *within* that one Meta package; do not treat them as instructions to produce two separate assets unless a genuine platform-fit reason requires the split (e.g. a format, caption length, or crop that only works natively on one destination).

### TikTok
9:16-first; native pace; readable captions; creator/product/scenario bias.

### Instagram Reels
9:16; polished but not sterile; strong cover frame.

### Instagram Carousel/Static
4:5 or supported native format; strong hierarchy; save-worthy education.

### Facebook Reels
9:16; can use slightly more contextual caption.

### Facebook Static/Carousel
Business context and clear takeaway.

### Threads
Creative may be text-only. Do not generate an image just to satisfy a visual quota.

## 12. Caption/Subtitles
- mobile readable;
- safe margins;
- high contrast;
- no excessive words per frame;
- caption styling should feel Cefflo, not template-marketplace;
- subtitle timing follows speech;
- verify names/product terms.

## 13. Audio / Voice
Use real Founder voice where strategically useful and consented.
Synthetic voice must not impersonate a real person without permission.
Music/sound must be licensed/allowed for intended commercial use.

**Voiceover cross-reference:** `10_BRAND_VOICE_LANGUAGE_SYSTEM.md` §20 Voiceover Direction governs delivery style specifically (avoid announcer/radio-ad energy, exaggerated enthusiasm, robotic pacing; conversational, natural pace, Malaysian rhythm for BM where applicable).

## 14. Creative Tool Router
Provider names belong in configuration, not doctrine.

Adapter classes:
- video_generation;
- avatar_creator;
- voice;
- image_generation;
- editing;
- captions;
- product_capture;
- design_render.

For every provider store:
- provider/model/version;
- cost;
- latency;
- commercial-use constraints;
- output quality notes;
- failure rate;
- supported aspect/duration;
- provenance where available.

## 15. Asset Multiplication
A real vendor/product shoot should maximize consented reusable assets:
- establishing;
- preparation;
- packing;
- ready;
- dashboard/phone;
- dispatch;
- rider pickup;
- handoff;
- founder/vendor explanation;
- real quote/testimonial;
- B-roll.

Do not assume "1 visit = 10–20 assets" is always achievable; treat it as a production target dependent on material/consent.

## 17. Creative Learning
Marketing Memory should record:
- opening frame;
- hook delivery;
- face/no-face;
- product-screen timing;
- duration;
- pace;
- caption density;
- visual pattern;
- CTA placement;
- production lane/provider;
- cost;
- performance.

Do not declare a creative rule universal from one winner.

## 18. Definition of Done
An approved experiment can be routed to the right production lane, rendered in platform-appropriate form, pass truth/brand/authenticity QA, retain lineage/cost metadata, and be replaced with another provider without rewriting marketing doctrine.
